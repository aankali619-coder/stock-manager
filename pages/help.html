❓ Help / Guide

Adding an item

Click + Add item, fill SKU, name, price, quantity, min stock (the alert level), and storage location. Save.

Recording stock changes

Click the ± button next to an item: in for new stock, out for sales/usage, adjust to set the exact count. Each change is logged with your name.

Scanning a barcode

Click 📷 Scan barcode and point your camera at the item's barcode — it searches automatically.

Low stock

Yellow rows are at or below their minimum. Use Show low stock to filter just those.

Roles

Admin: manage users and delete items. Staff: add/edit items and adjust stock.
