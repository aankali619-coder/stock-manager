📦 Stock Manager — About

What it is

Stock Manager is a lightweight inventory system for tracking items in a shop, warehouse, or storeroom. It records what you have, how much you have, what it costs, and where it is stored — in real time.

Who it's for

Small businesses, retail shops, school/lab stores, and anyone who needs to know their stock level without paper records. It runs in any browser — phone, tablet, or PC.

How it works

  1. Sign in (email/password or Gmail)
  2. Add items with SKU, price, quantity, min-stock, and shelf location
  3. Record stock coming in/out — every change is logged with who did it
  4. Get warned when items run low
