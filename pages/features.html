✨ Features

  • Item details — SKU, name, category, barcode, price, storage location
  • Stock tracking — add, remove, or set exact quantities; full history per item
  • Low-stock alerts — items at or below minimum show highlighted + filterable
  • Barcode scanning — use your phone camera to look items up fast
  • Search — find items by name, SKU, barcode, or category
  • Users & roles — admins manage users/delete items; staff handle daily stock
  • Secure sign-in — bcrypt passwords, session tokens, Gmail sign-in
  • Dashboard — total items, units, stock value, low-stock count
